Our Product

Two grades of sulphate of potash, matched to how your market applies it — field spreading or fertigation.

Granular SOP — 0-0-50

For direct field application on wheat, maize, cotton, sugarcane, vegetables and orchards. Slow, steady potassium release with added sulphur.

  • K₂O ≥ 50%
  • Granule size 2–4 mm
  • Low chloride — protects fruit quality
  • Improves size, colour and shelf life

Water-Soluble SOP — 0-0-52

Fully soluble powder for drip irrigation and foliar feeding — the premium grade for high-value fruit, vegetable and tobacco production.

  • K₂O ≥ 52%
  • 100% water soluble
  • For fertigation and foliar spray
  • Fast uptake at flowering and fruiting

Our Product

Two grades of sulphate of potash, matched to how your market applies it — direct field spreading or fertigation.

FIELD APPLICATION

Granular SOP — 0-0-50

For direct field application on wheat, maize, cotton, sugarcane, vegetables and orchards. Steady potassium release with added sulphur for grain filling and fruit quality.

  • K₂O ≥ 50% · Sulphur ≥ 17%
  • Granule size 2–4 mm, easy spreading
  • Low chloride — protects quality crops
  • Improves size, colour and shelf life
FERTIGATION · PREMIUM

Water-Soluble SOP — 0-0-52

Fully soluble grade for drip irrigation and foliar feeding — the premium choice for high-value fruit, vegetable and tobacco production.

  • K₂O ≥ 52% · Sulphur ≥ 18%
  • 100% water soluble, no residue
  • For drip systems and foliar spray
  • Fast uptake at flowering and fruiting

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions every importer asks first.

What is the minimum order quantity?

One full container — 20 tons. This keeps freight cost per ton at export level and matches standard distributor stock lots.

What are the payment terms?

T/T (bank transfer) or irrevocable L/C at sight, as agreed on the proforma invoice before loading.

How is quality guaranteed?

Every shipment carries a Certificate of Analysis issued against the specification on this page. The COA, invoice, packing list, bill of lading and certificate of origin are shared with you as a complete document set.

How long does delivery take?

Sailing time depends on your destination port and is confirmed in writing with your quotation, together with the loading port and shipment window.

Can bags carry our own brand?

Yes — neutral and buyer-branded 25 kg / 50 kg bags can be arranged on request. Share your artwork when you send your inquiry.
